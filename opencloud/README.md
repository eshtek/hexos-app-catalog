# OpenCloud

Files for your household, on your own server, in your browser. Open it with Launch. The first time, your browser warns about the certificate: accept it once.

**Signing in.** The sign-in you chose while installing is OpenCloud's administrator, and the only account it starts with. Make accounts for other people in OpenCloud itself, under Admin settings, Users.

**Where files are.** Each person's files are in the Documents folder, in `opencloud/users/<their username>-<short id>`. Spaces you share are in `opencloud/projects`. Anyone with access to Documents can see all of these over the network. Treat them as read-only there: OpenCloud may not notice a change made that way, and it can't save over a file someone put there.

**New documents.** "New documents are made as" sets what the New menu creates. You can change it on the Settings tab. Every format still opens.

**Editing documents.** Install Euro-Office or ONLYOFFICE and connect it from OpenCloud's Connections tab. There's nothing to copy.

**Calendars and contacts** work with any CalDAV or CardDAV app, and you can turn them off on the Settings tab.

**Search** finds file names. "Search inside documents" also finds words inside files. It's off unless you turn it on, since it uses about 1 GB more memory.

**Web apps.** Choose extra apps like Draw.io or Maps while installing, or later on the Settings tab.
